# Coverage Status Definitions

- **Planned:** hypothesis or artifact idea documented; not yet implemented or tested.
- **Implemented:** artifact exists and has been reviewed; not necessarily validated against real telemetry.
- **Lab-validated:** tested against documented lab data with reproducible results.
- **Production-observed:** telemetry evidence exists in an authorized production environment; publication must be sanitized and approved.
- **Tuned:** known false-positive patterns and exclusions are documented and reviewed.
- **Retired:** no longer relied upon; reason and replacement are recorded.

Confidence describes the strength of available evidence, not severity. Record the test scope, date, data sources, and limitations.
